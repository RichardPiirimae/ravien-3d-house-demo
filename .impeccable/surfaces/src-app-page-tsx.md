# Surface brief: home (/)

Scope: the single landing page. Mode: Persuade. The live model is the proof and leads the first viewport.
Audience: architects, developers and property marketers deciding whether to commission a 3D web build. Action: email Ravien ("Start a project").
Constraints: phones (touch orbit vs. page scroll), one WebGL canvas, and no invented clients, prices or testimonials.

## Direction contract (rev 2, pinned by the user 2026-09-27)

THESIS: A calm, professional construction-company site. The realistic, daylit model of the real building is the hero, and the UI gets out of its way. This replaces the drawing-set world, which the user found messy.

OWN-WORLD: Warm off-white ground #f5f5f3 that the canvas horizon matches exactly. Ink #151617, muted #5b5f64, hairlines #e2e2de, and one fir-green accent #1f4d3a for primary actions. Type is Archivo with a wide display cut (wdth 112). Buttons and floating toolbars are rounded pills on white with a soft offset shadow. Section cuts fill solid charcoal.

STORY: Visitors see a real building rendered live, cut it open, and walk into apartments. They believe Ravien can do this with their drawings, then start a project.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The headline "Your building, live on the web." sits bottom-left. The realistic aerial render fills the right 60%. A floating pill toolbar sits bottom-centre of the model area (Aerial/Street/Plan · Render/White/Lines · Explode), with the compass dial bottom-right. A fixed header carries the nav and the green "Start a project" action.

SIGNATURE: The compass dial is the building's footprint with a green view cone. Drag it to walk the camera around. Picking an apartment cuts the model at 62% of the storey height and isolates the apartment's footprint.

FORM: User-pinned direction (no roll). Supersedes seed e83a124b.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
